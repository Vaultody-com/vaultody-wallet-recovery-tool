Recover your vault without depending on anyone.

A standalone, offline tool that reconstructs your VAULTODY vault's master private key on your own machine. Open source, auditable, and it never opens a network connection.

How the backup flow works

  1. Pick a password, or generate a complex one under Generate password.
  2. Use it in Generate RSA key to produce a public key and a password-encrypted private key.
  3. Keep that password — without it the private key cannot be decrypted.
  4. In your VAULTODY Dashboard (open dashboard) create or open a vault and press Vault backup in its settings, then paste the public key into both RSA key fields.
  5. Store the private key offline — separately from the password. It is required to recover.
  6. Finish the backup in the Dashboard. The PDF it downloads documents the recovery process.

Embedded wallets: the 24 recovery words

  1. A vault made inside an app that embeds VAULTODY is backed up from the wallet itself: it shows 24 recovery words once, and e-mails you an encrypted backup file (.json).
  2. Keep the words on paper and the e-mail apart. Neither is any use without the other.
  3. To recover, open Recover with words, choose the backup file and type the words. The master keys are rebuilt here, offline.
